Project Overview

A 900 sq ft office that feels more alive than most spaces three times its size.

Reception & Work Area - Suspended black steel planter frames run the full length of the office — greenery cascading overhead, blurring the line between workspace and living environment. Concrete textured walls, walnut desks, terrazzo floors. Raw and warm at the same time.

Conference Room - A walnut vertical slat wall backlit in amber anchors the room. Linear baffle ceiling with integrated LEDs, dark sintered stone table, geometric steel base. The kind of room where decisions feel considered.
