Cookie Policy

EFFECTIVE DATE: JULY 10, 2026

Orizons Architects and Builders ("we," "our," or "us") uses cookies on orizons.in. This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, which ones we use, and how you can manage your preferences. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site to remember information about your visit — such as your preferences — making your next visit easier and the site more useful to you.

Cookies can be first-party (set directly by orizons.in) or third-party (set by services we use, such as Google Analytics). They can be session cookies (deleted when you close your browser) or persistent cookies (stored for a defined period).

2. Cookies We Use

We use two categories of cookies on this site:

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are required for the website to function. They do not require your consent under applicable law and cannot be disabled through our cookie banner.

Cookie name Set by Duration Purpose
orizons_consent orizons.in (first-party) 1 year Stores your cookie consent preference so the banner does not reappear on every visit.

Analytics Cookies

These cookies are only set after you explicitly accept analytics cookies through our banner or preference centre. They help us understand how visitors interact with the site so we can improve it.

Cookie name Set by Duration Purpose
_ga Google Analytics 4 (third-party) 2 years Assigns a unique ID to distinguish users and count visits. Helps us measure overall traffic and behaviour.
_ga_22SYNHXC59 Google Analytics 4 (third-party) 2 years Persists session state across page requests for this specific GA4 property.

3. How We Use Analytics Data

With your consent, Google Analytics 4 collects anonymised data about:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Device type, browser, and operating system
  • General geographic location (city/region level — no precise GPS)
  • Traffic source (how you found us — search, direct, referral)

We use this information solely to understand which parts of the site are most useful and to improve our content and navigation. We do not use analytics data to personally identify you or to run targeted advertising campaigns.

IP addresses are anonymised before being sent to Google. We do not share raw analytics data with any third party other than Google under its data-processing terms.

4. Managing Your Preferences

You have full control over analytics cookies at any time:

  • Cookie banner — shown on your first visit; choose Accept all, Reject all, or Cookie settings for granular control.
  • Preference centre — reopen at any time using the button below or the "Cookie settings" link in our footer. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it.
  • Browser settings — most browsers let you block or delete cookies. Note that blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect site functionality.
  • Google opt-out — install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on to prevent data collection across all sites using GA.

5. Third-Party Cookies (Google)

Google Analytics 4 is operated by Google LLC, 1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA. When you consent to analytics cookies, data is transferred to and processed by Google in accordance with its own privacy terms.

For more information on how Google uses data, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google Analytics data practices.

6. Data Retention

Analytics data is retained in our Google Analytics property for 14 months before automatic deletion. Your consent preference (stored in the orizons_consent cookie) is retained for 1 year from the date it was set, after which we will ask again if you revisit the site.

7. Your Rights Under the DPDP Act 2023

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request erasure of your data where we have no legitimate reason to retain it
  • Withdraw consent at any time — this does not affect the lawfulness of any processing already done

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at connect@orizons.in.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when we add new cookies, change third-party providers, or when the law requires it. The effective date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision. For material changes, we will reset your consent preference so the banner reappears on your next visit.
