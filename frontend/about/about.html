WHO WE ARE

Designed from the ground up.

Orizons is a complete architecture and design studio based in Jaipur & Aurangabad, working with residential and commercial clients across India.

We handle architecture, interior design, construction management, and solar panel installation under one roof, with one team, and with total accountability at every stage.

We were founded by architects who chose to build something rather than take the easier path. Our approach is straightforward: rigorous design, honest execution, and a single point of contact from first idea to final finish.

Every project at Orizons is handled directly by the founding team. The people you hire are the people who show up.

THE PEOPLE

Trained at the top.
Built from the ground up.

Our studio is led by Principal Architects trained at Kamla Raheja Vidyanidhi Institute for Architecture and Environmental Studies (KRVIA), Mumbai, one of India's most respected architecture schools.

We are a focused studio with strong fundamentals and no inherited bad habits. New enough to bring fresh thinking. Trained rigorously enough to back it up.

THE REASON

We started this because someone had to.

Construction and design in India are full of compromises, on quality, on aesthetics, on honesty with clients, and on the environment. We started Orizons because we saw what good architecture could do for a home, a business, a family, and how rarely people actually got it.

Our work is not just about beautiful spaces. It is about buildings that are responsibly designed, correctly executed, and built to last. With solar integration now part of our practice, it is also about buildings that generate their own energy, reducing costs and environmental impact from day one.
